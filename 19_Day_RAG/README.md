# 📘 Day 19 — RAG: Grounding AI in Documents

**Concept:** Models hallucinate; documents don't. RAG = retrieve the facts, generate *from* them, cite sources.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — A Q&A bot over a real PDF. Specify chunking and what 'I don't know' looks like.
3. **Evaluate** — 5 questions, every answer checked against the source. One unanswerable question - it must admit ignorance.
4. **Business lens** — 'Chat with our documents' is the #1 requested AI feature in businesses. You can now scope and price it.
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
