# 📘 Day 21 — Project 3: A Live AI Product

**Concept:** Composition: everything from week 3 as one working system.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — Deploy a RAG chatbot over real documents. Spec first: what it knows, refuses, and what success looks like.
3. **Evaluate** — Stranger-test it. Note confusions. Add 'try these questions' prompts.
4. **Business lens** — A demo you can show a paying client next week. Price it in your head: what would you charge a business?
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
