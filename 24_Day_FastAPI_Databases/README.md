# 📘 Day 24 — Backends: APIs & Persistence

**Concept:** Frontends are faces; backends are the business. An API is a *contract* other software builds on.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — A FastAPI backend: POST /ask, feedback storage, stats. Spec the endpoints first.
3. **Evaluate** — Hit it with curl. Break the contract (wrong fields) - does it reject cleanly?
4. **Business lens** — APIs are products too - many AI businesses *are* APIs. Contracts and uptime are the business model.
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
