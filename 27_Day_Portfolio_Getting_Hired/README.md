# 📘 Day 27 — Packaging: Portfolio & Getting Paid

**Concept:** Skill that can't be *seen* can't be *bought*. Packaging = making your work legible to non-technical buyers in 60 seconds.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — AI drafts your GitHub profile and project READMEs; you rewrite every line until a non-technical friend 'gets it.'
3. **Evaluate** — The 60-second test with a non-technical person. Can they say what you do and why it matters?
4. **Business lens** — List 10 companies hiring AI/FDE roles. Map your 3 projects to their requirements - that's your pitch.
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
