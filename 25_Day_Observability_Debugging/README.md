# 📘 Day 25 — Operations: Logging & Runbooks

**Concept:** You can't fix what you can't see. Observability = the system telling you its story.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — Request IDs, latency + token + cost logging, a simple dashboard. Specify the 5 questions logs must answer.
3. **Evaluate** — Answer 'what happened yesterday at 3pm?' from your logs. Write the runbook.
4. **Business lens** — Uptime is a promise you sell. Runbooks let a one-person operation keep promises like a team.
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
