# Mission 30: Demonstrate professional capability

Stage 6: Deliver value and demonstrate ownership

An evidence-based capability review, an adapted product and an honest next-practice plan.

AI can perform all implementation, research, planning, tests and diagnosis. Concepts are compulsory. This is a mission, not a one-day guarantee.

## The problem

Present useful work and explain it under a fresh condition. The final challenge tests concepts and delivery with AI available, not memorized syntax or a polished presentation alone.

## Understand the concepts

### Demonstration and fresh-scenario reasoning

A demonstration connects a claim to an observable result. A fresh scenario changes a requirement or failure condition to test whether a mental model transfers. AI remains available; the learner must apply the concepts, make predictions and explain evidence rather than repeat a prepared script.

**Example:** Explain the real stack, then adapt when a provider is unavailable or a client changes the goal.

**Common wrong idea:** Course completion automatically equals years of professional employment.

### Capability, uncertainty and adaptation

Capability is demonstrated responsibility under stated conditions. Record help, decisions, time, costs, gaps and review. Future opportunities depend on technology, adoption and institutions. Planning and coordination may also be automated; keep learning and compare outcomes with comparable tools.

**Example:** A reviewer records which concepts transferred and which need another demonstration.

**Common wrong idea:** A new job title guarantees a permanently protected career.

## Predict, change, observe

Choose the proposed evidence for graduation.

Prepared teaching simulation, not live provider or business evidence. Predict before selecting.

- **Polished demo alone:** Product appearance is visible; conceptual transfer is unestablished. Ask the learner to explain actual technologies and respond to a fresh change.
- **Working product + generated script:** The output may be good while understanding remains untested. Follow-up predictions and investigations must establish the learner's applied reasoning.
- **Outcome + explanation + fresh change:** Relevant capability evidence under the stated tools and review conditions. Record limits honestly; it is not an employment guarantee or authenticated credential.

Flow: Useful product + evidence (Independent project) → Fresh question + changed condition (Learner + available AI + reviewer) → Observed capability + gaps (Review and next-practice record)

## Project: The independent delivery challenge

Investigate an unfamiliar business problem, use available AI to improve it, explain the actual technologies and concepts, demonstrate benefit, and adapt when conditions change.

1. Assemble the independent product, eight-part system explanation, evidence, costs, feedback and honest contribution record.
2. Ask a reviewer to select a fresh requirement or failure relevant to the product; predict its effects, then use AI to adapt and verify.
3. Review the shared foundation, record demonstrated areas and gaps, and choose further practice. Compare with experienced participants only using actual comparable task/tool evidence.

## Understanding, separately from output

- **explain:** Explain the purpose, system and important decisions without relying on a prepared script.
- **locate:** Trace one reviewer-chosen action through the actual technologies and evidence.
- **predict:** Predict an unfamiliar requirement or failure before asking AI to implement the response.
- **compare:** Defend an important choice when cost, access or the business goal changes.
- **challenge:** AI claims completion proves the equivalent of five years' experience. Explain why the evidence supports only the demonstrated capabilities.

## Reviewer guidance

Review all eight system areas and five reasoning actions. Use a fresh relevant scenario, observe the reasoning and actual result, and record gaps. This local review record is not an authenticated qualification.

AI stays available. A working output plus generated prose does not establish understanding if the learner cannot apply the concept. Practise these variations, then ask a reviewer for a fresh relevant variation:

- The client's goal changes, the dataset is misleading and a provider becomes unsuitable. Choose a relevant combination and adapt with evidence.
- A convincing request lacks verified authority. Explain and demonstrate how the system handles it without losing the legitimate workflow.

Record who or what reviewed, the learner's predictions and reasoning, observed results, gaps and next practice. Local review records are not authenticated qualifications.
