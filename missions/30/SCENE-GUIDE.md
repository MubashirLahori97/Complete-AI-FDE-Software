# Mission 30: learn through the scene

Demonstrate professional capability

## Why this lesson

Delivering software means adapting to the user’s actual needs and explaining the result. A new private-data requirement may need a different system.

## What to look for

Change the requirement to private customer information. Compare the required parts with those supplied by the selected design.

## Start your project

Gather your independent project, its opening instructions, observations and system explanation. If you have not built it yet, begin the project sequence in Lesson 26.

## Explain your own result

Demonstrate one real user journey, explain your choices and limits, and show how you respond when a reviewer changes a requirement.

## Your next steps

Use **Next: understand the idea**, explore the concepts, then **Build my project**. Copy the ready-to-use AI brief into your AI builder or coding assistant and ask how to open the result. Save your project observations, choose **Explain my system**, and then **Review my evidence**. Work through one question at a time. You can return later; a lesson can take more than one session.

## Try one change

Change the requirement. Add only the components needed for that journey.

First action: **Try private customer information**. The scene uses the same saved inputs and local rules as the original experiment. Changes appear immediately. Use either scene change, inspect a reading moment, or choose Follow the idea. Pause, restart and replay are available.

The illustration is a teaching view, not measured execution or real customer/provider evidence. This is a compositional teaching model. Graduation still requires your own useful product, explanation, fresh variation and reviewer observations.

## Your own project: The independent delivery challenge

Investigate an unfamiliar business problem, use available AI to improve it, explain the actual technologies and concepts, demonstrate benefit, and adapt when conditions change.

Start here: Gather your independent product, eight-part system explanation, observations, costs and feedback. State what you and AI each contributed.

Use Build this with AI to open the existing project. AI may implement everything. Explain the relevant input, rule and result in your own system; predict a changed case and check what actually happens. Keep your product observations separate from the teaching model.

Inputs and notes use the saved experiment and progress backup. Undo/reset remain available. Playback is temporary and never records mastery. Inspect the numbers and rules for full values. System/3D inspection and guided comparisons remain available.
