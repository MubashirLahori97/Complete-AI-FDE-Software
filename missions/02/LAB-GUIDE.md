# Mission 2: interactive experiment

Turn a feeling into a direction.

## Start here

Open mission 2 in the academy. Describe one change to the hours or Services section. Include an observable check.

First action: **Try a bounded direction**. Change the controls and observe the result. The browser executes a local teaching model; it does not contact a customer system or an AI provider.

## The idea

A builder needs an observable change. Name the part, describe what should happen, and say how you will check it. A bounded instruction leaves fewer unrelated decisions open. This does not guarantee that any particular AI will follow it.

## Things you can change

- **Your direction to the builder:** starts at "Make it impressive.".
- **Allowed scope:** starts at "all". Choices: One part; The whole site.

## A different situation

Write a different instruction that another person could check without asking what impressive means.

Predict the result before trying it. Record the value you changed, what actually happened and why. Inspect the system parts in 3D or use the equivalent flat diagram. Rotation and separation change your view; the lesson controls change the model.

## What this experiment establishes

This builds and inspects a brief locally. It does not call or evaluate an AI builder. The checklist recognises a few example terms, not the quality of arbitrary writing.

Your controls and notebook are saved in this browser and included in a progress backup. The experiment JSON export records the model inputs and output. It is separate from real project evidence and understanding review. Continue to Understand, then Build with AI to create your own project using MISSION.md and AI-BRIEF.txt.
