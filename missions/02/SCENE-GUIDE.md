# Mission 2: learn through the scene

Turn preferences into clear direction

## Why this lesson

‘Make it impressive’ leaves a builder guessing. A clear request names one change and a way to check it.

## What to look for

Compare the vague request with the example. Find the named section, the change, and the check. The local checker only recognises a few phrases.

## Start your project

Look at a page and name one confusing part. Write one change you want AI to make and how you will check the result.

## Explain your own result

Explain the visitor’s problem, your exact request, and what you observed after the change.

## Your next steps

Use **Next: understand the idea**, explore the concepts, then **Build my project**. Copy the ready-to-use AI brief into your AI builder or coding assistant and ask how to open the result. Save your project observations, choose **Explain my system**, and then **Review my evidence**. Work through one question at a time. You can return later; a lesson can take more than one session.

## Try one change

Describe one change to the hours or Services section. Include an observable check.

First action: **Try a bounded direction**. The scene uses the same saved inputs and local rules as the original experiment. Changes appear immediately. Use either scene change, inspect a reading moment, or choose Follow the idea. Pause, restart and replay are available.

The illustration is a teaching view, not measured execution or real customer/provider evidence. This builds and inspects a brief locally. It does not call or evaluate an AI builder. The checklist recognises a few example terms, not the quality of arbitrary writing.
