# Mission 28: learn through the scene

Respond to reality

## Why this lesson

An improvement helps only if people use it and the work saved outweighs new work it creates. A faster step can still make the whole process worse.

## What to look for

Reduce correction work and compare the net time saved. Also check how many people actually use the change.

## Start your project

Choose a small, agreed pilot for your project. Decide who will try it, what result you will measure and what would make you stop or revise it.

## Explain your own result

Explain what happened in use, how it compared with the earlier process, and which feedback led to your next change.
