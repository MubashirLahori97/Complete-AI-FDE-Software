# Mission 18: learn through the scene

Add an AI product capability

## Why this lesson

An AI answer can look neatly formatted while inventing a fact. A repair-note helper must keep an unknown date unknown.

## What to look for

The example replaces the invented date with null, meaning ‘unknown’. Compare the format checks with the separate check against the supplied facts.

## Start your project

Choose one useful job for a note assistant. Write what information it may receive, what it should return, and what it must do when information is missing.

## Explain your own result

Explain why your feature uses AI, which facts support its answer, and how you handle missing or unsupported information.

## Your next steps

Use **Next: understand the idea**, explore the concepts, then **Build my project**. Copy the ready-to-use AI brief into your AI builder or coding assistant and ask how to open the result. Save your project observations, choose **Explain my system**, and then **Review my evidence**. Work through one question at a time. You can return later; a lesson can take more than one session.

## Try one change

First action: **Keep the unknown date unknown**. The scene uses the same saved inputs and local rules as the original experiment. Changes appear immediately. Use either scene change, inspect a reading moment, or choose Follow the idea. Pause, restart and replay are available.

The illustration is a teaching view, not measured execution or real customer/provider evidence. You are running a real JSON parser and a small source-checking rule on fictional input. No model is called and this does not measure general factual accuracy.
