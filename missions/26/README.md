# Mission 26: Start an independent domain project

Stage 6: Deliver value and demonstrate ownership

A domain-grounded project brief with a discovered requirement and measurement plan.

AI can perform all implementation, research, planning, tests and diagnosis. Concepts are compulsory. This is a mission, not a one-day guarantee.

## The problem

Transfer the concepts to an unfamiliar workflow: building maintenance, a clinic's non-clinical administration, logistics, education or another authorized domain. A familiar demo is not evidence that you understand this setting.

## Understand the concepts

### Domain constraints and incentives

Domain knowledge explains the real work and its limits. Incentives explain why people may adopt, avoid or work around a change. The person requesting the app may not be the person doing the extra work. Investigate both perspectives.

**Example:** A manager wants more completed visits; technicians need travel and access time counted fairly.

**Common wrong idea:** A reusable app template establishes the requirements of every business.

### Success agreement and uncertainty

A brief records the problem, source evidence, scope, authority, constraints and success conditions. Uncertainty belongs in the brief as questions and assumptions. AI can research and prepare it, but invented details must stay labelled.

**Example:** Separate an observed missed handoff from a guess about its annual cost.

**Common wrong idea:** A detailed AI-written proposal makes an unverified workflow factual.

## Predict, change, observe

Choose what you discover during a maintenance-workflow investigation.

Prepared teaching simulation, not live provider or business evidence. Predict before selecting.

- **Manager wants faster dispatch:** A goal to examine with the people doing the work. Speed may conflict with equipment, travel or access constraints.
- **Technician lacks access approval:** A missing requirement that changes the workflow and acceptance criteria. A new scheduling screen alone would not resolve the blockage.
- **AI estimates savings:** A hypothesis requiring assumptions and measurements. Generated numbers are not observed business benefit.

Flow: Unfamiliar workflow (Domain participants / authorized sources) → Evidence + missing requirement (Investigation) → Agreed or labelled brief (Project decision record)

## Worked example

**before:** Reuse the appointment app unchanged in a building-maintenance company.

**action:** Investigate a recent visit and discover site-access approval and equipment qualifications.

**after:** The brief changes the allowed assignments and definition of a preventable missed visit.

**lesson:** The concepts transfer; the domain assumptions require fresh evidence.

Authored example; your project requires its own observations.

## Project: An independent outcome brief

Investigate an unfamiliar authorized domain and define a useful improvement with participants or a clearly labelled scenario. Include local language, connectivity, cost and data constraints.

1. Choose a real-world setting, what you have permission to access and where you can gather evidence. Use AI to help research and prepare questions.
2. Draw the current workflow. Explain why people would use or avoid a change, and find one requirement missing from the first request.
3. Compare buying a tool, configuring one, connecting services and building something. Agree the useful result, scope and measurement plan, or clearly label practice assumptions.

## Understanding, separately from output

- **explain:** Explain what domain understanding changed in your initial idea.
- **locate:** Find the source behind the discovered requirement and an important unknown.
- **predict:** What if the person entering data gains extra work but no benefit?
- **compare:** Compare the selected approach with buying or configuring an existing product.
- **challenge:** AI says an old demo proves this workflow's business case. What fresh evidence is needed?

## Reviewer guidance

Introduce a plausible domain constraint omitted from the brief and ask the learner to revise scope and success.

AI stays available. A working output plus generated prose does not establish understanding if the learner cannot apply the concept. Practise these variations, then ask a reviewer for a fresh relevant variation:

- The workflow must support low connectivity and two languages. Revise the brief.
- The measured delay includes customer cancellations. Reassess the outcome definition.

Record who or what reviewed, the learner's predictions and reasoning, observed results, gaps and next practice. Local review records are not authenticated qualifications.
