# 📘 Day 30 — Ship It: Demo & Next 90 Days

**Concept:** The story of the work is part of the work. Retrospectives turn experience into judgment.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — A 3-minute demo video (you present; AI helps script) + written retrospective + next-90-day plan.
3. **Evaluate** — Name what broke, what you'd do differently, which concept was hardest and why.
4. **Business lens** — Next 90 days: ONE depth area + ONE way to get paid. Write it down. Commit publicly.
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
