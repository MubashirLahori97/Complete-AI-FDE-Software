# 📘 Day 26 — Discovery: Problems into Specs

**Concept:** The most valuable skill here: *discovery*. Clients describe pains, not specs. You translate pain into buildable, priced scope.

**Time:** ~60–90 minutes. **Rule:** explain the concept in plain words, or the day doesn't count.

---

## The loop today

1. **Learn** — the concept above. One focused idea; own it 100%.
2. **Specify** — Interview a real small business for 20 minutes (AI helps *prepare* questions). Write a one-page brief: problem, users, in/out of scope, price.
3. **Evaluate** — Is the brief clear enough for a stranger to build v1? What's deliberately excluded - and why?
4. **Business lens** — This IS the business: discovery -> scoped brief -> price -> build -> deploy. The FDE loop in one day.
5. **Commit** — push today's work. Your history is your proof.

> AI writes the draft. **You** specify, evaluate, and stand behind it. Never ship output you can't explain or defend.
